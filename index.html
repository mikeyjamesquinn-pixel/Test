Departure board settings

To show live trains, this board needs a free key from National Rail’s Rail Data Marketplace:

  1. Register at raildata.org.uk and choose “Individual not affiliated with a company”.
  2. Subscribe to the free Live Departure Board product.
  3. Go to View subscriptions → Live Departure Board → Specification and copy the Consumer key.

Approval can take up to 3 working days. Tap Show demo to try the board meanwhile.

Station codes: Reigate REI, Redhill RDH, London Bridge LBG, Victoria VIC. Leave “Change at” blank for a single board, and “To” blank to show every train.
